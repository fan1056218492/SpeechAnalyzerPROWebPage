Privacy policy

Transcription and translation stay local.

Last updated: July 4, 2026

App privacy

SpeechAnalyzer PRO does not upload your audio, transcript text, or translations. The app is designed around Apple SpeechAnalyzer and Apple Translation frameworks running on your Mac.

System models

Language and translation models are managed by macOS System Settings. The app checks model availability but does not download models itself.

StoreKit exception

Apple StoreKit purchase, restore, and price-loading flows are Apple system purchase flows. The app does not add its own network API, analytics, or remote logging.

Website privacy

This static website does not include analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms, cookies, or online fonts. Cloudflare may process standard request logs as the hosting provider.
